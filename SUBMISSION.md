# Silly Kitty

**You only move the robot. Kitty decides the rest.**

Kitty is out in the garden again, and kitty does not take orders. All you have is a little hovering robot that kitty finds endlessly interesting. Fly it around, keep kitty's attention, and lure it home to its bed before the timer runs out.

Kitty has a mind of its own:

- It **follows the robot** while it can see it, and stops to stare when it can't.
- It **hates water**: one step into a puddle or a sprinkler's spray and it's game over.
- It **can't resist yarn**, and will drop everything to play with it.
- It **runs from** the robot lawnmower, the dog (once it wakes up) and a sprinkler about to spray, and doesn't look where it's running.
- Leave it alone for too long and it **dozes off**.

15 handmade levels, each adding a new twist: puddles, yarn, robot lawnmowers, a sleeping dog, sprinklers and, finally, all of them at once. Finish fast to earn up to 3 stars per level. Your progress is saved in your browser.

## Controls

- **Move the robot:** arrow keys, WASD, or a gamepad's d-pad or left stick.
- That's it. There are no other buttons, not even in the menus: to start, just move. Failed levels restart and cleared levels move on by themselves.

## The restriction: Movement Input Only

The only input is moving the robot, so menus are rooms you fly through, restarts are automatic, and you never press anything but a direction. Every challenge comes from where you put the robot, because the cat (an AI) decides what to do about it.

## How AI was used

Every part of Silly Kitty was made with Claude (Anthropic), used through Claude Code. No asset packs and no other AI tools; apart from the font, everything was written for this jam:

- **Design ideation:** proposed the game concepts (the "laser lure" idea won) and how each level introduces one new twist.
- **Code:** all ~2,200 lines of GDScript in Godot 4.7.1. That includes kitty's brain, a utility-scoring state machine (chase, play, flee, nap, go to bed) with line of sight, a nap meter and give-up rules. It's classic game AI and runs offline; no language model runs inside the game.
- **Art:** all 31 sprites, written by hand as SVG code and animated part by part in the engine (tail sway, ear twitches, the hop when turning round, wheel and walk cycles), and the whole garden (striped lawn, hedges, trees, picket fence, benches) drawn by code.
- **Sound:** all 12 sound effects, generated by a Python synthesiser Claude wrote (the meow is a little vocal-formant model).
- **Music:** both chiptune loops, composed as note patterns in a Python synthesiser Claude wrote, then encoded to MP3.
- **Levels and balancing:** the 15 layouts, plus a scripted "bot" solution for each level that the automated tests fly. Every time limit is measured from those runs, so each level is provably solvable and the difficulty curve stays smooth.
- **Quality:** 55 automated gameplay test cases (109 checks), a checker that enforces the movement-only restriction on every script and scene, and browser tests of the web build.

## Content

No intense content: no horror, gore, flashing imagery or heavy themes. Failing a level makes the room give a short, mild shake.
